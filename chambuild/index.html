About CHAMBUILD

Built on purpose.
Driven by people.

We bring a considered, collaborative approach to construction, engineering and property improvement in The Gambia.

FOUNDER & CEO | CHAMBUILD LTD

Hamidou Cham

Hamidou Cham is the founder of Chambuild, a Gambian construction company established in 2024 and built on quality construction, professionalism and long-term investment in the built environment.

Trained in civil engineering and construction management, Hamidou brings hands-on construction experience from his time working in the United States, together with an international education, home to The Gambia.

For Hamidou, construction is more than putting up buildings. It is creating homes, strengthening communities, generating employment and raising standards across the industry.

His vision is simple: to help build a stronger Gambia, one project at a time.
